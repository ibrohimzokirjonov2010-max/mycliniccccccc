- Subtitle (30): `Dental clinic CRM`

Full description:

My Clinic is a CRM for dental clinic staff.

The app lets staff:

- sign in with a clinic ID, username, and password
- see today’s visits, revenue, and debt
- add a patient and open the record
- book, complete, or cancel a visit
- view the doctor list
- record a cash, card, or transfer payment
- change the password and request account deletion

A clinic administrator creates the account. The app does not register new accounts. Patient data stays on the clinic server and is not used for advertising.
